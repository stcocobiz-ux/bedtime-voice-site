Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bedtime Voice: Parent Stories (“Bedtime Voice,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), operated by Haiyun Peng, collects, uses, shares, retains, and protects information.

1. Scope

This Policy applies to the Bedtime Voice iOS app, its backend services, related support communications, and the public pages that link to this Policy.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect or process:

  • Account information: your Sign in with Apple user identifier and, if Apple provides them and you choose to share them, your name or email address.
  • Device and app information: device-binding identifiers, app version, operating-system version, device model, locale, and related operational metadata.
  • Voice and audio content: guided voice recordings made inside the app, temporary voice-processing artifacts, voice-preview audio, and generated story audio.
  • Purchase information: App Store product identifiers, purchase intents, transaction-verification facts, entitlement status, refund or revocation facts, and restore-purchase facts. Apple controls payment-card and App Store billing information.
  • Usage and operational information: consent records, generation status, playback eligibility, deletion requests and status, service errors, security events, audit records, and provider-usage records.
  • Product interaction and analytics information: first-party records of interactions such as viewing the story catalogue and full-pack offer, starting or completing a story sample, starting or completing a purchase, and opening purchased story text. These records may include pseudonymous event or journey identifiers, timestamps, story, pack, language and product identifiers, and restricted account or device linkage used for validation, attribution, security and deletion. They do not include recorded voice audio or full story text.
  • Support information: information you choose to include when contacting support.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • authenticate users and maintain account and device access;
  • record and verify voice-processing consent;
  • create a short voice preview and generate purchased bedtime story audio;
  • store generated audio privately and provide authorised playback;
  • process purchases, restore eligible purchases, and handle refunds or revocations;
  • operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve reliability of the service;
  • measure how features such as story exploration, sample playback, purchases, and story-text access are used and how effectively those product flows operate;
  • respond to support requests;
  • process account-deletion and cleanup requests; and
  • meet legal, accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, dispute, audit, refund, and App Store reconciliation obligations.

4. Voice and AI processing

Bedtime Voice is a parent-facing service. Voice samples must be recorded live through the app's guided flow. The service may send the audio, story text, and limited processing metadata needed for voice generation to a third-party AI or voice-generation provider.

This processing is performed to provide app functionality. It is not used by Bedtime Voice for advertising or tracking. Bedtime Voice does not provide users with a permanent, independently reusable voice model.

5. How we share information

We may share or process information with:

  • Apple: for Sign in with Apple, StoreKit purchases, purchase verification, refunds, revocations, and restore behavior;
  • Cloud infrastructure and storage providers: to host the backend and privately store recordings and generated audio;
  • AI and voice-generation service providers: to create temporary voice-processing artifacts, previews, and story audio;
  • Professional advisers and authorities: where reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and security, resolve disputes, or prevent fraud.

Bedtime Voice uses its own first-party backend for this product analytics and does not use a third-party analytics provider for these events.

We do not sell personal information. Under the current service design, we do not use advertising SDKs, ad measurement, data-broker sharing, IDFA, or cross-app tracking.

6. Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy. Voice recordings, temporary voice artifacts, demo audio, and generated audio follow their applicable service and deletion lifecycles. Cleanup can be asynchronous and retryable.

Raw and account-linkable product analytics records are retained for 90 days from the time of the event. We do not retain permanent analytics aggregates from these Story Visibility events under the current service design.

Some transaction, entitlement, refund, restore, financial, audit, security, deletion, support, and operational records may be retained where reasonably necessary or legally required for accounting, tax, fraud prevention, disputes, refunds, App Store reconciliation, support, and proof of deletion processing.

7. Account deletion and privacy choices

You may request account deletion using the in-app account-deletion feature where available or by contacting support.bedtimevoice@gmail.com.

Account deletion may remove or anonymise account profile information and queue cleanup of voice recordings, temporary voice artifacts, demo audio, and generated audio. Required financial, transaction, refund, fraud-prevention, audit, dispute, restore, and reconciliation records may be retained.

When an account-deletion request is processed, analytics events and journey mappings linked to that account are deleted without waiting for the normal 90-day analytics retention period. This does not change any separate retention that may apply to transaction, financial, refund, fraud-prevention, audit, dispute, Restore, legal, or App Store reconciliation records.

Restoring an eligible purchase after account deletion does not restore old personal information, prior voice recordings, or previously generated audio.

8. Security

We use technical and organisational measures designed to protect information, including authenticated backend access, private object storage, and time-limited authorised playback access. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Bedtime Voice is designed for parents and guardians. It does not create child profiles and is not intended for independent use by children. Adults are responsible for the recordings, purchases, and permissions submitted through their accounts.

10. International processing

Information may be processed in countries other than your own where our service providers operate. Those locations may have different data-protection laws. We use service providers and safeguards appropriate to the nature of the processing.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the service, law, or data practices. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice where required.
