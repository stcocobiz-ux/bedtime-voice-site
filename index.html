Bedtime Voice: Parent Stories

Bedtime Voice is a parent-facing iOS app that helps parents create private bedtime story audio using their own recorded voice.

The app uses a guided in-app recording flow, a short voice preview, one-time story-pack purchases, private audio storage, and authorised playback.
