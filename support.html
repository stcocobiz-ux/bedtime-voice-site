Bedtime Voice Support

For help with Bedtime Voice: Parent Stories, email support.bedtimevoice@gmail.com.

When contacting support, include the app version, your iPhone model, the approximate time the issue occurred, and a brief description of what you saw. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, raw voice recordings, Apple receipts, signed transaction data, transaction identifiers, or private playback links.

Frequently asked questions

Sign in with Apple is not working

Confirm that your device is connected to the internet and that you are signed in to iCloud. Close and reopen the app, then try again. Bedtime Voice does not use a separate password.

The app cannot access my microphone

Open iPhone Settings, find Bedtime Voice, and confirm that Microphone access is enabled. Voice samples must be recorded live inside the app; external audio files cannot be imported.

My voice preview or stories are still processing

Voice and story generation may take time. Keep the app installed and check the relevant screen again later. If the status remains unchanged for an extended period, contact support with the approximate time and the stage shown in the app.

I completed a purchase but do not see my story pack

First use Restore Purchases in the app. If the pack still does not appear, contact support. Do not send Apple receipts or signed transaction information by email.

How do refunds work?

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Refund requests are handled through Apple's purchase-support process.

How do I restore purchases?

Open the app's Library or Settings area and choose Restore Purchases. Eligible non-consumable story-pack purchases can be restored to the signed-in account. Restore does not recreate prior voice recordings or generated audio that was removed as part of account deletion.

How do I delete my account?

Use the account-deletion option in the app where available. Deletion and cleanup may be asynchronous. Certain transaction, financial, fraud-prevention, dispute, audit, refund, and App Store reconciliation records may be retained where reasonably necessary or legally required.

How is my voice used?

Your guided recordings are used to create a short preview and generated bedtime story audio. Processing may involve a third-party voice-generation provider. Bedtime Voice does not provide you with a permanent, reusable voice model.
